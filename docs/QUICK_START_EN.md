# First launch

1. Extract the **entire** Windows ZIP into a new folder and run `AlbionCraft.exe`.
2. Choose **Language / Язык → English**. Your choice is saved automatically.
3. Select the server (Europe / Americas / Asia), city, item family, tiers, enchantments and quality. Click **Update prices**. Profiles are separate per server; your game server must match.
4. Check the age and source of quotes. **Unknown** means missing information;
   **≤** marks an upper bound before unknown expenses.
5. Open **Trips** in the left sidebar, choose buying and refining cities, available carry weight,
   purchase budget and resources. Enter the station fee in silver per 100 nutrition.
   The station fee is already calculated: do not add it again under other costs.
6. Choose profit in silver or ROI and run **Find maximum profit**. Compare the
   **Without Focus** and **Allocate Focus** plans. Review the shopping list,
   market volumes and costs before buying.

## Stock, orders and local observations

**Stock / bank reader** distinguishes stock already at the refining destination
from prior purchases in the buying city. Destination stock needs no transport;
prior purchases count toward weight without being bought again. Enter actual
acquisition costs where possible; a saved market estimate is labelled as such.

For optional local market observations, click **Connect client…**. Network capture
requires Npcap, installed through its own official wizard. Use one client at a
time, then open market and own-order pages in the game. Order creation/cancellation
requires server confirmation; simply opening the order form does not create a row.
Some events cannot identify an order: reopen the own-order pages to reconcile it.

For bank reading, select only complete visible cells. Scroll with overlapping rows
and confirm the container is fully scanned only after reaching the bottom.
Recognition runs locally. Check uncertain quantities and item matches.

## Loadouts and progression

**Builds / market** compares activity builds against available quotes. Your personal
loadout can use cheaper tier/enchantment alternatives at the target item power.
Bags and capes have separate targets; missing prices remain unknown.

**Progress / journal** opens the Destiny Board. Left-click to edit a node and
right-click to go back. **Combat Fame Credits** can calculate a single item's
level or allocate credits across a branch. Calculation previews do not change
your profile until you record a completed action.

Filters, exclusions, language and haul presets are saved. Personal character,
container and preset names keep the spelling you entered.

## Updating and personal data

For farming, open **Island**, choose the city, product and number of plots.
Set Premium, mastery, specialization and available Focus; check feed per animal.
Update prices and calculate. Profit is for a repeated harvest cycle with seeds or
offspring kept for the next cycle. Initial purchases appear in a separate card.
It covers selling crops or grown animals, not milk, eggs, butchering or saddling.

Extract a new release into a new application folder. The Windows build reuses
your data in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\AlbionCraft`; you do not need to copy settings into
the program folder. The source installation keeps data beside its source.

This is a beta. Quotes, history and observed order pages can be incomplete or
outdated; profit and sale times are estimates. Other-city comparisons use the
same cargo and exclude a second delivery. The application does not perform
in-game actions. See [privacy](../PRIVACY.md) and [notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
